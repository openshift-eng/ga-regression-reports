Total regressions increased 26% while unique JIRA bugs more than doubled (+103%), continuing the trend from 4.21 of improved triage granularity — regressions are increasingly attributed to distinct root causes. Resolution times improved modestly (avg -10%, P95 -5%), but triage times increased (avg +27%, P95 +53%). The most notable improvement is in regressions open on the report date, which dropped 92% from 12 to 1, and total SBARs dropped from 6 to 2 (-67%). However, one of the two SBARs (OCPBUGS-64847) is a carryover from 4.21 that has now been SBAR'd across two consecutive releases — see Prior Release SBAR Follow-up below.

**OCPBUGS-64847 remains unresolved after spanning two full release cycles (4.21 and 4.22).** This bug was first detected in November 2025 during 4.21 development and was SBAR'd as a Candidate for 4.21 GA. It has now been SBAR'd again — this time as Approved — for 4.22. The bug remains in POST status with an upstream fix still pending merge in kubernetes/kubernetes. The upstream PR ([#136508](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/pull/136508)) has been LGTM'd but is waiting on the 1.36 release. This represents a serious gap in regression resolution that requires immediate attention and a clear plan to prevent a third consecutive SBAR in 5.0.
