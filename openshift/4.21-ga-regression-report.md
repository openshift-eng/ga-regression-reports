**Note:** The 4.21 release produced 6 total SBARs (3 from triaged regressions + 3 additional exceptions) where 4.20 had none. While the SBAR-per-100-jobs ratio remains under 2%, the presence of multiple SBAR exceptions in a single release warrants attention. The dramatic drop in total regressions (-69%) alongside a large increase in unique JIRA bugs (+214%) suggests improved triage granularity — more regressions are being attributed to distinct root causes rather than bulk-triaged. The increase in regressions open on the report date (+140%) reflects both expanded job coverage (+36%) and more persistent product issues requiring SBARs. P95 time to triage improved significantly (-52%), but P95 time to resolve increased sharply (+110%, from 8.7 to 18.3 days), indicating that while most regressions are triaged faster, the hardest-to-fix regressions are taking much longer to close.
